03 / ABOUT ME

A creative mind.
A technical curiosity.

I'm Kai Plunkett, a second-year Creative Media (Games Design) student interested in what makes games feel alive — from the way a story unfolds to the sounds and systems that bring it together.

My work combines interactive storytelling, sound editing, music production and developing programming skills. I'm especially interested in the technical side of game audio and how sound can respond to what players do.
